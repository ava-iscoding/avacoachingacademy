BRAND POSITIONING

Ava Coaching Academy is founded around the idea that life never stops moving.

Time continues.

Human beings move through different stages, identities, relationships, environments, emotional states, worlds, and levels of awareness.

I believe that human beings can connect almost like the wagons of a train—each person, stage, relationship, lesson, and experience becoming connected to another as we move forward through life.

We move through transitions.

We leave one world and enter another.

We change internally and externally.

We grow through different stages of consciousness, understanding, relationships, careers, emotional experiences, spiritual awareness, and identity.

This movement from one level of existence and understanding into another is part of what I see as ascension.

Ava Coaching Academy exists to help people move through those transitions consciously rather than remaining trapped in fear, confusion, emotional blocks, limiting beliefs, past experiences, or environments they have already outgrown.
